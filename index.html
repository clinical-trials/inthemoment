Meet the Band Director

Calvin Appleberry

For 16 years, Calvin Appleberry has been one of Albuquerque's most sought-after pianists, with a sound that lives where sophisticated jazz meets classic soul. Leading his own ensembles, his polished, room-filling touch has carried him from Vernon's Speakeasy to Governor Michelle Lujan Grisham's Inauguration Ball.

"Whether I'm playing a quiet private dinner for two or a ballroom full of people, my job doesn't really change. I absorb the feeling in the room and play to it."

"I'm here to be heard, not seen. The night isn't about me; it's about you. I'm the ambiance, and your enjoyment is the main attraction. I'm here to support your event, not to take over your event."

Bride Guide

Classic ceremony songs for the walk in and the walk out.

For wedding clients, these familiar processional and recessional selections are a helpful starting point for choosing the bride's entrance, family entrance, and ceremony exit music.

Your wedding music can go far beyond jazz. In the Moment performs classical ceremony pieces, pop, R&B, Motown, country, hip-hop, EDM, and more. Because every celebration brings together different generations and traditions, we work with each couple to create a custom mix that feels personal, cohesive, and right for every moment of the day.

01

Mendelssohn's Wedding March

A bright, traditional recessional choice for the couple's ceremony exit.

02

Bridal Chorus

The classic bride entrance piece, often known as "Here Comes the Bride."

03

Canon in D

A graceful and widely loved choice for processionals and family entrances.

04

Prince of Denmark's March

A stately trumpet-style selection for a formal and elegant ceremony moment.

05

Ode to Joy

A joyful, uplifting selection that works well for exits and celebratory transitions.

06

Jesu, Joy of Man's Desiring

A warm, reverent choice for processionals, seating, and ceremony atmosphere.
